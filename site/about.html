Hosting great events since 1998.

A family-run venue with a big-event team — we look after every detail so you can enjoy the day.

Our story

ACME Venue began as a single lakeside function room and has grown into one of the region's most sought-after event destinations.

Today we host conferences, product launches, weddings and community celebrations for more than 1,200 clients each year. Our in-house chefs, event coordinators and technical crew work as one team from first enquiry to final invoice.
